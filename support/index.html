Support

Your notes are yours. We never sell them or use them for ads. They're encrypted, used only to run Noodle, and you can delete everything anytime. Read the privacy policy.

How do I sign in?

Enter your email and Noodle sends you a one-time code. Type the code in and you are in. There is no password to remember.

The code didn't arrive

Check your spam or junk folder first. If it isn't there, wait 60 seconds and tap resend for a fresh code. Make sure the email address is spelled correctly.

How do I edit or delete a note or person?

Open the person or note, then tap Edit. From there you can change it or delete it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete account. You can also follow the steps on the delete account page.

Where is my data stored, and is it sold?

Your notes are stored encrypted on our cloud database provider. We never sell your data or use it for ads. Voice is turned into text on your device when your phone supports it. Otherwise your phone's speech service (Apple or Google) does it, and Noodle never stores recordings. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Still stuck?

Email us at support@noodleit.me and we will help.
