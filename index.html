How it works

Turn everyday moments into better connections.

Tell Noodle about the people in your life. The things they care about, what you're working on together, and the conversations you don't want to lose. Noodle connects the dots, brings the right details back when they matter, and helps you turn what you know into thoughtful ways to reach out.

Your people vibe

See the strengths you bring to your relationships.

The little things you do shape how people experience you. Noodle helps you recognize your natural strengths, understand how you connect, and discover where a small shift could make an even bigger difference.

Questions

Good to know

The basics

What is Noodle, exactly?

A small memory for the people in your life. You tell it what you learn about someone, and it keeps those details on that person's card. Each day it turns them into a few ideas for reaching out: a follow-up, a birthday, a link they'd love.

Is this a CRM?

No. There are no fields to fill in, pipelines or follow-up quotas. Noodle is built for friends, family and the colleagues you actually care about, and it works from the way you'd naturally talk about them.

How much time does it take?

A few seconds after you see someone, and a minute or two a day if you want to act on an idea. Noodle shows a handful of cards each day, not an inbox to clear.

Does it cost anything?

No. Noodle has no paid features or in-app purchases right now.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Both. The iPhone app is coming soon to the App Store.

How do I sign in?

Enter your email and Noodle sends you a one-time code. No password to remember.

Using Noodle

Does Noodle message people for me?

Never. Noodle suggests; you decide. Tapping Text, Call or Email opens your own phone app with a draft you can change or ignore.

What's a connection card?

When someone has an interest you've mentioned (sourdough, trail running, a band), Noodle sometimes finds an article or idea they'd enjoy. You can send it to them, or tell Noodle "more like this" or "not for them" so the next one fits better.

Will it bombard me with notifications?

No. Noodle keeps it gentle: usually one short morning note, and you can turn notifications off anytime.

What are Spark, Ally, Champion, Rock and Bridge?

Five ways people show up for each other. Noodle notices which ones your reach-outs lean toward, so you can see your strengths, plus one gentle idea for room to grow. It's a mirror, not a score.

Can I keep notes about myself?

Yes. You have your own card for the things about you, like goals and what you're up to.

Can I fix something Noodle got wrong?

Yes. Open the person or note, tap Edit, and change or delete it.

Privacy and AI

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Your notes are encrypted, used only to run Noodle, never sold and never used for ads.

How does Noodle use AI?

To sort what you say onto the right person, write summaries and daily ideas, and answer questions you ask about your people. Note content goes to Anthropic, our AI provider, only when you use those features, and Anthropic doesn't train its models on it.

Does Noodle read my contacts?

No. If you link someone to a contact so you can text or call them from Noodle, you pick that one contact, and it stays on your phone. It's never sent to our servers.

What happens to my voice?

It's turned into text on your phone when your phone supports it, otherwise by Apple's or Google's speech service. Noodle never stores recordings, only the text.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete account, or follow the steps on the delete account page. Your data is deleted, generally within 30 days.

Does Noodle track my location?

No. Noodle doesn't collect location data.
