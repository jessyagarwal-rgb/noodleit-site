Questions

Good to know

How do I sign in?

Enter your email and Noodle sends you a one-time code. No password to remember.

Does Noodle message people for me?

No. Noodle suggests; you decide. Tapping Text, Call or Email opens your own phone app with you in control.

What happens to my voice?

It's turned into text on your phone when your phone supports it, otherwise by Apple's or Google's speech service. Recordings are never stored.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open your profile and choose Delete account, or follow the steps on the delete account page.
